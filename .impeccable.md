## Design Context

### Current status: A selected for whole-app implementation; release not accepted

On **2026-09-23**, the owner explicitly selected **"Develop A — Academy
weekboard"**. This authorizes the complete real-app implementation across the
frozen product scope, not another prototype or a Home-only slice. It is concept
selection, **not final integrated visual acceptance or permission to deploy**.
The candidate must remain on a clean, backed-up held branch with no PR while the
parent coordinates independent code/functional and visual craft review, followed
by the owner's integrated-app acceptance.

**Integrated implementation candidate, 2026-09-23:** the held branch uses the
Academy shell and replacement tokens across all primary workspaces and connected
supporting routes, introductory/settings surfaces and shared native dialogs.
Training, match and reflection saves use atomic local record/XP updates with
retained drafts on failure. The existing multi-squad controls now address every
selected squad through the existing endpoints; an in-page retry skips squads
whose response was already confirmed. The reviewed v2 validator and draft helper
are pinned in the project. [Implementation notes](docs/academy-implementation.md)
describe the migration and remaining verification/acceptance boundaries.
Historical concept captures are not evidence for this implementation.

On **2026-09-22**, after PR #10 shipped, the owner rejected its aesthetic quality
and the untouched remainder of the application. A functional post-training slice
did **not** meet the requested site/app redesign outcome. Passing checks did not
establish design acceptance.

The owner selected the new lead character: **Football academy — bold sports
typography, strong player identity, tactical graphics and purposeful layouts;
energetic, not childish.** This is confirmed context, not a new agent inference.

The audience remains **players aged 10–14, with parents supporting**. The
confirmed Golazo palette remains **cobalt blue + warm coral with neutral
surfaces**. It is not a universal lab palette or a ban on green. Product identity,
club accents, lab identity and semantic statuses remain separate.

### Product job and scope

Support the whole football week: prepare for training and matches, record what
happened, reflect, practise and learn, understand recorded progress, and maintain
a personal football identity. Parents support without reading private diary
content; coaches have purposeful squad/session workspaces.

All five player tabs — **Home, Log, Progress, Learn, Profile** — belong to the
same design task. So do their drillthroughs, settings/onboarding, dialogs and
role-specific surfaces. No blanket “all other pages remain legacy” exclusion.

The source-controlled scope is [`.design-scope.json`](.design-scope.json),
with kind `product-redesign`. It records the actual owner request and assigns
distinct stable IDs to pages, detail views, dialogs/wizard steps and their role
contexts. The treatment groups below do not replace that inventory. A scope
declaration is not approval of every proposed page or of either concept.

The deliverable is a **full integrated, buildable real-app candidate** implementing
A across the inventory: shared responsive shell, all five main tabs, details,
dialogs, onboarding/settings, calendar/tournaments and coach/mentor experiences.
Existing capabilities, schemas, authentication, XP/age rules, localization and
local-save/cloud-sync distinctions must remain intact. No unrelated backend,
feature or loading-optimization expansion is authorized.

### Concept decision and preserved alternatives

- **A — Academy weekboard (lead):** a persistent academy rail, spatial week
  planning, a date-stamped training action and original tactical practice
  graphics. Log is a selector beside the record; progress is an inspectable
  histogram; Learn is a filtered practice library; Profile is an original
  academy registration card.
- **B — Player journal:** horizontal matchday navigation, a dated match/session
  spread, journal tools, a wide activity trace, a featured-practice spread and a
  player dossier. Same data and capabilities; not a recolored A.

Neither option uses commercial football-game cards, mascots, player photos or
borrowed assets. Local system/Academy typography is chosen for this character,
not from a font ban. Neither reuses Rosette’s serif/plaster identity.

**A — Academy weekboard is selected, 2026-09-23.** B remains historical comparison
material, not an implementation target. Production must use real data, not the
concept's synthetic records or illustrative rewards.
See [the interactive comparison](docs/design-directions/academy-20260922/README.md)
and [whole-app coverage](docs/design-directions/academy-20260922/COVERAGE.md).

### Healthy motivation and truthful data

Effort matters; rest and missed sessions are normal. No shame, streak-loss
pressure, fabricated urgency, talent prediction or invented recommendations.
Charts explain recorded activity and distinguish sparse history from a trend.
XP is secondary activity feedback, not ability or worth.

Concepts use an explicitly fictional Player 07 and academy scenario in Latvian
and English. All state is in memory and resets on reload. Saves/rewards are
labelled simulations; no authentication, APIs, storage or sync are exercised.
Existing training/match/diary fields and age/reward semantics constrain later
implementation. Mentor views must not reveal diary text.

The inventory distinguishes rendered concepts, individually mapped treatments,
unchanged behavior boundaries, defined-but-unwired routes and unimported
components. For example, current coach statistics contain placeholders; the
concept must not present them as a working analytics service.

### Verification and acceptance

Exercise real controls, navigation, failure/retry, sparse/populated data,
language/long text, dialogs and representative role flows at narrow and desktop
sizes. Check native keyboard/focus, contrast, reduced motion and source-bound
captures. A contact sheet supports comparison; screenshots alone do not prove
the interactions work.

Technical checks are evidence of bounded behavior, not proof of visual quality,
real-user usability or an “award-winning” result. No independent aesthetic
acceptance or representative-player research is claimed by a passing script.
The owner may choose a direction, request combinations, or reject both.
The character choice and concept selection do not establish integrated quality.
The real application must communicate its quality directly. New implementation
evidence uses the merged v2 contract, but craft review and integrated owner
acceptance remain unverified until actually obtained. No passing approval
receipt, PR, merge or deployment is permitted from this implementation session.

Earlier concept captures remain bound to their recorded source revision.
Adding this source inventory does not silently rebind them to a new source
commit or make them pass the new scope-aware approval contract.
