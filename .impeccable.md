## Design Context

### Status and decision

**Audience confirmed by owner, 2026-09-22:** players aged 10–14, with parents
supporting. **Visual direction: PENDING owner choice between A and B.**
This is a bounded concept exercise, not approval to redesign production.
No direction was selected by the implementing agent.

### Primary job

Finished training → log it quickly → understand progress → leave encouraged.
Design for short, mobile-first visits after football training, including a
parent's phone. Supporting parents are not an audience for surveillance or
comparison with other children.

### Personality and healthy motivation

Encouraging, capable, personal. Effort matters; rest and missed sessions are
normal. No guilt, shame, streak anxiety, fabricated urgency or compulsory return.
XP acknowledges a recorded activity, not football ability, health or worth.
Celebrate a meaningful completion once; never turn every tap into a reward.

### Two proposed directions

- **A — Clubhouse:** belonging through a fictional club identity, an original
  jersey/crest, expressive type, a clubhouse noticeboard and earned celebration.
  Navy, mint and warm coral are a proposal, not an approved palette. No public
  team feed, fabricated teammate activity, ranking or social pressure.
- **B — Training companion:** focused athletic typography, restrained surfaces,
  a linear training journal and one clear next action. Ink, chalk and lime are
  a proposal, not an approved palette. Progress is explained rather than scored
  against other players.

Both use the same synthetic Alex / Northbank FC U13 training scenario and
interactive states. Neither copies commercial football-game or language-app
assets. Neither transplants Rosette's serif/plaster identity.
See [the rendered directions and verification](docs/design-directions/training-20260922/README.md).

### Journey and states

Initial scheduled training; editable log; field validation; simulated waiting;
completion with comprehensible progress; rest/missed training without penalty;
simulated save failure and explicit retry preserving input; first-use preview.
Date, type, duration, energy, mood, focus areas and notes reflect existing
`TrainingEntry` fields. Proposed rest acknowledgement is not a new production
training type or a backend integration.

### Constraints and verification

Native keyboard controls, visible focus, comfortable targets, reduced motion,
WCAG 2.2 AA as the accessibility target, 320/390px mobile and desktop reflow,
200% text enlargement and long-string stress checks. Concepts use installed
local fonts, no dependencies, downloads, authentication, API calls, storage or
sync. All content/results are labelled synthetic/simulated; reload resets them.
Do not enter real player details.

Production supports multiple languages through react-i18next; concept copy is
English with an explicit Latvian long-string stress fixture, not a complete
localization. No production performance budget is approved by this exercise.
Measure concept transfer size and requests without claiming field performance.

### Approval boundary

Production source, backend, theme, progression logic, dependencies and CI remain
unchanged until owner selection. Independent review is arranged separately;
automated/browser checks are not real-user testing or owner approval.
After selection, observe the task with a willing player and appropriate parent
support before making usability-research claims.
